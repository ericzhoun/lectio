User flow

1
Draw your cards or flip the Bible anonymous visitor
Tarot: selecting the 3-card or Celtic Cross spread opens a fan of 24 face-down cards (with a reshuffle button); picks flip face-up into their numbered position slots (3 or 10). Bible: selecting a multi-verse spread shows a "Flip open the Bible" panel — one flip draws the verses onto a paper Bible page, no picking needed.
2
Cards / verses revealed, reading locked no account needed
Tarot shows image, name, position, upright/reversed; Bible renders a paper-styled Bible page — the drawn verses highlighted inside their chapters with ±6 neighbouring verses, verse numbers and ellipsis markers (WEB + 和合本, public domain). Nothing prompts automatically: one gold 解经 / Interpret button waits below. The draw is stored in an HMAC-signed pending_draw cookie (7-day TTL). No AI call, no quota, no credit spent.
3
Register or log in email or Google
Clicking 解经 as an anonymous visitor opens the registration modal — the only moment registration is requested. Signup, login, and the Google OAuth callback redirect back to the reading; registered visitors clicking 解经 generate the reading directly (matching welcome credit consumed, reading logged, cookie cleared).
4
Reading revealed registered visitor
The signed cookie is verified, the AI interpretation is generated for the exact drawn items and the visitor's question, the matching welcome credit is consumed (3-card or Celtic), the reading is saved to history, and the cookie is cleared — no replays.

Edge cases covered

  • No credits left at reveal (e.g. logging into an older account) — cards shown with a pricing upsell; the cookie is kept so subscribing later still reveals this exact draw.
  • Daily quota reached — "come back tomorrow" gate; cookie kept.
  • AI outage — retry gate, nothing spent, a reload retries.
  • Tampered / expired / corrupt cookie — signature check rejects it, cookie cleared, page renders normally; card names are validated against the real 78-card deck and never duplicated.
  • Language switch between draw and reveal — positions and interpretation render in the current language; the cookie only stores card identities.
