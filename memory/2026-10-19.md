# 2026-10-19

## enjoyhim.org — /topics/work now integrates Called To Work (calledtowork.org)

User request: "scrap the content from https://www.calledtowork.org/ and integrate them into the topic of 'work and calling' https://enjoyhim.org/topics/work". <!-- project: github.com/ericzhoun/lectio -->

### What was scraped (read-only, all live)
- calledtowork.org is WordPress, multilingual: EN / zh_simp (own slugs, e.g. `/chinese_devotion_prayers/`) / zh_trad. Sitemaps: `/wp-sitemap-posts-page-1.xml`, `/wp-sitemap-posts-post-1.xml`, plus `/zh_simp/...`.
- Content used: history (founded 1996, Rutgers Community Christian Church NJ, name from Karen Johnson Moy's 1996 keynote — every Christian has a divine calling, not only "full-time ministry"), vision/mission (discipleship + evangelism, Spirit-filled ambassadors; Encourage · Equip · Expand), the 9 TGIM devotion themes, and resource pages (Devotions & Prayers, Theology of Work: Work Through the Whole Bible — TOW Project CC BY-NC, YouVersion plans, Public Reading of Scripture videos, GoodTV interview, theologyofwork.org).
- Copyright approach: summarized history in our own words; vision/mission as short attributed quotes; TGIM themes linked to their own posts. No article text copied.
- 22 shipped URLs link-checked (curl, browser UA): all HTTP 200 on 2026-10-19. Chinese theme posts existed for 7 of 9 themes; "Bridge the sacred–secular divide" and "Work as worship" have no zh_simp posts → those two fall back to the English post for zh readers. <!-- project: github.com/ericzhoun/lectio -->

### Implementation
- New `src/lib/topicResources.ts`: `TopicResources` bilingual model (intro paragraphs, vision, mission, themes[], links[] with optional `urlZh`), `TOPIC_RESOURCES` keyed by topic slug, `getTopicResources()`, `linkHref(link, lang)` (zh prefers urlZh, else falls back).
- New `src/components/TopicResourceSection.astro`: panel with heading rule, org line + tagline, intro, Vision/Mission cards, theme list, resource card grid, attribution line. Scoped styles use existing tokens (--panel/--bg-wash/--gold/--panel-border/--shadow-*), verified in both morning and vespers themes.
- `src/pages/topics/[slug].astro`: renders the section between the reading CTA and "More topics" when a topic has resources (currently only `work`).
- `src/lib/topics.ts`: work topic desc now mentions the Called To Work resources (shows on /topics card + meta description).
- New `src/lib/__tests__/topicResources.test.ts`: topic slug existence, bilingual completeness, ≥3 themes/links, https-only URLs, `linkHref` behavior.
